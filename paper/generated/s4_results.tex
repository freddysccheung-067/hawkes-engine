The primary confirmatory result is that the reported standard errors understate
sampling spread. Pooled over the twelve two-type cells with at least 2000 events,
$q = \HsixQ{} \pm \HsixQSe{}$, and the four-type satellite points the same way
(\HsixSatQ{} $\pm$ \HsixSatSe{}). Per block, over the same cells, $q$ is
\HsixQMu{} $\pm$ \HsixQMuSe{} for the baselines, \HsixQAlpha{} $\pm$
\HsixQAlphaSe{} for the excitation entries, and \HsixQBeta{} $\pm$ \HsixQBetaSe{}
for the decay rates. The shortfall is therefore concentrated in the decay, and it
is concentrated in the tail: a median-based companion gives \HsixQRob{} $\pm$
\HsixQRobSe{}.

At $N \ge 10\,000$ every cell's coverage lies between \HsixCovLargeLow{} and
\HsixCovLargeHigh{}, so the shortfall is a small-$N$ phenomenon rather than a
property of the estimator at all sample sizes.

Two hypotheses were refuted, and both refutations are informative.
\HsixThreeLabel{}: coverage was predicted to fall monotonically toward
criticality, but it rises from \HsixThreeLowRho{} at $\rho = 0.3028$ to
\HsixThreeHighRho{} at $\rho = 0.60$. \HsixFourLabel{}: timestamp quantisation
was predicted to bias the decay estimate upward, and at $N \ge 10\,000$ it moves it
downward.

The conditioning verdict separates coverage, but mostly mechanically:
\HsixMechanicalShare{}~percent of \texttt{unreliable} replications carry no
standard error at all, so they
cover nothing by definition. Among those that do carry one, the separation is
\HsixPostHoc{}. The verdict marks fits that produced an interval; it does not
detect an interval that is wrong.
